A MORE PERSONAL NOTE

What separates a memorable birthday note from a forwarded text

For a close friend, refer to a shared joke or a moment you still laugh about. For a family member, say what you value about them beyond the occasion. For a colleague, keep the message encouraging and respectful. If you are posting publicly, avoid mentioning someone’s age or personal plans unless you know they are comfortable with it.

  • Use the recipient’s name and mention one quality you value about them.
  • For a close friend, a shared memory can make a simple wish feel original.
  • Keep workplace greetings warm and respectful, especially when sharing in a public team channel.

RELATIONSHIP TONE MATRIX

How to adapt a birthday wish for milestones, belated notes and tough years

Not every birthday calls for high-energy confetti. When someone is celebrating a major milestone (such as an 18th, 30th, 50th or retirement birthday), acknowledging the chapter they are entering adds weight to your note. If you are sending a belated wish, skip lengthy excuses and focus on making the recipient feel valued today.

When someone had a difficult year

Avoid pressuring them to “party hard.” Instead, wish them a gentler year ahead, steady strength and people who stand beside them without judgment.

In office & team channels

Focus on reliability, positive energy and professional growth rather than personal jokes or references to age.

Formatting your birthday card for WhatsApp and print

A birthday card works well with the recipient’s name, one clear wish and a short sign-off. Choose a design that suits their style, check that the message remains legible on a phone, and export the finished card as a PNG.
