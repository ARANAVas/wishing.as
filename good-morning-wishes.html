MESSAGE ETIQUETTE

सुबह का संदेश समय और रिश्ते के हिसाब से भेजें

A good-morning message should feel like a small kindness, not a request for an immediate reply. Think about the recipient’s routine before sending it: a friend may enjoy a cheerful note, while someone with an early shift, caregiving duties or a different time zone may prefer messages later in the morning.

A useful morning wish can be one sentence. Avoid sending repeated greetings to someone who rarely responds, and keep work-group messages within the group’s normal communication hours. If the recipient is facing a difficult day, a supportive line is often more helpful than telling them to “stay positive.”

  • For a family group: choose a warm wish that does not require everyone to answer.
  • For a close friend: mention a small plan or something they are looking forward to.
  • For a colleague: keep it brief and send it during reasonable work hours.
  • For someone under pressure: offer encouragement without making assumptions about how they feel.
