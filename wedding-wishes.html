Wedding wishes for a beautiful beginning.

A wedding message should celebrate the couple and the life they are beginning together. Whether you are writing to a close friend, a family member or a colleague, choose a message that feels sincere and appropriate to your relationship.

Writing for the couple’s personality and your closeness to them

A wedding message should celebrate the couple without making assumptions about their plans, beliefs or family roles. Choose a tone that suits your relationship: personal for close friends, warm for relatives and concise for colleagues or acquaintances. A few well-chosen words are often better than a long formal speech.

A MORE PERSONAL NOTE

What to write on a wedding envelope, guestbook or WhatsApp card

If you know both partners, address them equally and mention a quality you admire in their partnership. Avoid unsolicited advice, jokes about marriage or assumptions about children, religion and future plans. For a public post, make sure the couple is comfortable with any photo or personal detail you include.

  • If you know both partners, address them equally.
  • Avoid assumptions about family plans, religion or traditions.
  • For a formal card, a concise message and your name are enough.

WEDDING CARD & SHAGUN ETIQUETTE

Tailoring wishes for the couple, their parents and formal receptions

In Indian weddings, greetings are often sent not only to the bride and groom, but also to the parents hosting the ceremony. When writing directly to the couple, address both names equally and celebrate their companionship. When congratulating parents on their son’s or daughter’s marriage, acknowledge the warmth of the family welcome and wish the new household harmony.

For a shagun envelope or gift

One warm sentence of blessing plus both partners’ names and your full family sign-off fits neatly without crowding.

For a colleague’s marriage

Keep the tone warm and respectful, wishing them a joyful ceremony and a restful break before they return.

Designing a keepsake wedding congratulation card

A wedding card should make both names and the congratulations easy to notice. Choose a design with enough space for the message, proofread names carefully, and check the final PNG before sending it to the couple.
