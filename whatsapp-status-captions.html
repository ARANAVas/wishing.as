MOBILE-FIRST CAPTION NOTES

Write for the phone screen, not only for the caption box

A status caption is often read quickly and may appear over a photo, video or interface controls. Keep the key thought short, use natural line breaks and leave visual space around the most important words. If the background is busy, place the text over a calm area or choose a design with stronger contrast.

A caption should describe or add meaning to the moment rather than repeat everything visible in the image. Before posting, check that names, locations, school or workplace details and other identifying information are appropriate to share.

  • For a story layout, keep essential words away from the top and bottom edges where app controls can cover them.
  • Use one clear idea per caption; a second sentence is useful only when it adds context.
  • If other people appear in the photo, get their permission before posting it publicly.
  • Preview the exported image on your own phone and check that Hindi characters and line breaks remain legible.
QUICK ANSWERS

Frequently asked questions

How long should a WhatsApp status caption be?

Keep it short enough to read at a glance. One clear thought usually works better than a long paragraph.

Can I use Hindi and Hinglish captions?

Yes. The examples include Devanagari Hindi, English and Roman Hindi.

Can WishingASR create a status image?

Exporting a 9:16 vertical status card that fits phone screens
