A LITTLE ABOUT US

Words that make
moments matter.

WishingASR is a browser-based greeting-card creator for festivals, milestones, and everyday affection. We aim to make it simple to choose an occasion, personalise a message in English, Hindi or Hinglish, and create a card to download or share.

AA Group is the parent company behind WishingASR. WishingASR is part of the AA Group portfolio, with a focus on creating thoughtful, accessible digital experiences for everyday celebrations.

Photos used in the greeting-card creator are handled locally in your browser for the card preview and export. WishingASR does not provide user accounts or a server-side greeting library in this static version.

What WishingASR helps you do

Visitors can browse occasion-based message collections, choose English, Hindi or Hinglish, and use the greeting creator to personalise a message. The card creator offers 20 designs and lets visitors preview lettering and layout before exporting an image. The site also includes a calendar, quote cards, captions, invitations, saved wishes and browser-based planning tools.

The tools are intended to help someone finish a real task: find a suitable starting line, edit it for a particular person, make a readable card, or save a reminder. They are not a substitute for checking important dates, local customs or the recipient’s preferences.

How the greeting creator works

Choose the occasion and language, review the suggested wording, then add a recipient name or your own sentence. Select a design and lettering treatment, and inspect the preview before exporting. Portrait, story, square, landscape and link-preview formats are available for different sharing contexts.

A selected photo is processed in the browser for the preview and card export. The current static version does not create a user account or upload a photo to a WishingASR greeting library. Saved wishes and optional preferences are kept in the visitor’s browser, not synced between devices.

How our message guides are prepared

The WishingASR editorial team organises examples around specific occasions, relationships and language needs. We use digital writing tools where helpful during drafting, then select and edit wording for clarity, tone and practical use. The interactive wish generator itself uses curated local templates and a visitor’s edits; it does not send each wish request to an AI service.

Examples are starting points, not quotations from real people, official translations or religious instructions. Hindi and Hinglish wording is intended to sound natural in context rather than mirror English word for word. A reader should adapt a message to the recipient, the family’s preferred form of address and local traditions.

Festival dates and observances can vary by region and year. The site calendar is a planning aid only; check a trusted local or official source before arranging ceremonies, travel or time off.

Corrections and ongoing maintenance

We aim to keep the creator, guides and date information understandable as the site evolves. A useful correction includes the page address, the phrase or feature that needs attention, and the suggested fix. Please report broken controls, incorrect language, confusing instructions, unreadable card layouts or outdated descriptions so the relevant page can be checked.
